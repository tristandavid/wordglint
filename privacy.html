Privacy Policy

WordGlint, by WealthBoard Apps · Last updated October 6, 2026

The short version: WordGlint has no accounts. Your progress, scores and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device for our server is a Friend Match you choose to start or join (a display name and the scores of that match, deleted automatically after 48 hours). We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

WordGlint ("the app", "we") is published by WealthBoard Apps. You can reach us at wordglintsupport@wealthboardapp.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Game progress: levels, XP, coins, hints, power-ups, badges, stickers, streaks, scores and your saved Daily Glint results.
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, theme) and which purchases you have made.

We do not receive this information. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we run.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, microphone or camera data. The app does not contain analytics or crash-reporting tools that send data to us. The only third-party SDK that communicates over the internet is the advertising SDK described below.

4. Purchases

Purchases (such as the season pass, VIP subscription, coin packs, the starter pack and Remove Ads) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment card details. Apple or Google may share limited purchase information with us, such as confirmation that a purchase or subscription is active, so we can unlock what you bought.

5. Advertising

WordGlint may show optional rewarded ads, for example to double coins or earn a bonus spin, from third-party advertising networks, through the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation service. When an ad is shown, LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad, according to its own privacy policy. On iPhone, you will be asked for permission before any cross-app tracking. You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads). Buying Remove Ads or VIP turns off these ads.

6. Sharing a challenge

If you tap Challenge after a round, the app makes a short code (the board and your score). It is shared only if you choose to send it, using your device's share sheet. The code contains no personal information.

7. Friend Match (online)

Friend Match lets you play the same board as a friend. It is the only feature that talks to a server we run (api.wordglint.wealthboardapp.com), and only when you create or join a match. When you do, we receive and store:

  • the display name you typed (it can be any name you like, 16 characters at most; please do not use your real name if you prefer not to);
  • a random player ID and secret token generated for that match, used only so that scores can be matched to the right player;
  • the match code, the board seed and mode, and the score and number of words each player submits.

This data is shown to the other player in the match (your display name and score). It is not linked to your name, email, device identifier or location, and it is not used for advertising or analytics. Matches and their data are deleted automatically after 48 hours. Our server may briefly process your IP address to deliver the service and limit abuse, and standard server logs may contain it; we do not use it to identify you. The server is hosted with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

8. Leagues and leaderboards

The rivals in weekly leagues and on the daily leaderboard are practice opponents generated by the game. They are not real people, and your score is not sent to other players.

9. Children

WordGlint is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control of it. Deleting the app removes it. Friend Match data on our server is deleted automatically after 48 hours, or earlier if you email us the match code. See how to delete your data. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their policies, and you can manage subscriptions in your store account.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example Canada, the EU/UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we hold almost no personal data (only the short-lived Friend Match data above), there is usually little for us to provide, but you can contact us at any time with a request or a question.

12. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security. Friend Match traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not run servers that hold your game progress.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data (for example, by adding online leagues), we will update this page and the date above, and where required, ask for your consent in the app.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy: wordglintsupport@wealthboardapp.com.
