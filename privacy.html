Privacy Policy

WordGlint, by WealthBoard Apps · Last updated October 7, 2026

The short version: WordGlint has no accounts. Your progress, coins and settings stay on your device. If you use online features (Friend Match, live duels, leaderboards, leagues or the duel lobby), your display name, avatar and scores go to our server so other players can see them. There is no typed chat: players only exchange preset reactions. We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

WordGlint ("the app", "we") is published by WealthBoard Apps. You can reach us at wordglintsupport@wealthboardapp.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Game progress: levels, XP, coins, hints, power-ups, badges, stickers, streaks, scores and your saved Daily Glint results.
  • Your settings (sound, music, haptics, theme) and which purchases you have made.

We do not receive this information. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we run.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, microphone or camera data. The app does not contain analytics or crash-reporting tools that send data to us. The only third-party SDK that communicates over the internet is the advertising SDK described below.

4. Purchases

Purchases (such as the season pass, VIP subscription, coin packs, the starter pack and Remove Ads) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment card details. Apple or Google may share limited purchase information with us, such as confirmation that a purchase or subscription is active, so we can unlock what you bought.

5. Advertising

WordGlint may show optional rewarded ads, for example to double coins or earn a bonus spin, from third-party advertising networks, through the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation service. When an ad is shown, LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad, according to its own privacy policy. On iPhone, you will be asked for permission before any cross-app tracking. You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads). Buying Remove Ads or VIP turns off these ads.

6. Sharing a challenge

If you tap Challenge after a round, the app makes a short code (the board and your score). It is shared only if you choose to send it, using your device's share sheet. The code contains no personal information.

7. Online play: Friend Match, live duels and the duel lobby

Online features talk to a server we run (api.wordglint.wealthboardapp.com), and only when you use them. Friend Match and live duels let you play the same board as a friend. A live duel is a turn-based match where you and a friend take turns finding one word each, so the words you play are sent to the server and shown to the other player. When you create or join a match we receive and store:

  • the display name you typed (any name you like, 16 characters at most; please do not use your real name if you prefer not to);
  • a random player ID and secret token generated for that match, used only so that scores and moves can be matched to the right player;
  • the match code, board seed, mode, live-duel format and level, and the score, number of words and, in live duels, the words played;
  • the quick reaction you pick (one of a fixed list of preset messages, such as "GG"). There is no free-text chat.

This data is shown to the other player in the match. Matches and their data are deleted automatically 48 hours after they are created.

Duel lobby (optional, off by default). If you switch on "Show me as available" on the Live duel page, other players who are also in the lobby can see your display name, avatar and duel level, and can send you a duel invite (an invite carries no message). You can switch this off at any time; you then stop appearing within about a minute. You can block any player (they can no longer see you or invite you, and you stop seeing them) and report a player's name; reports are reviewed by us and kept for up to 12 months. Blocking is stored against your anonymous player ID.

8. Leaderboards and leagues

The weekly league, the daily leaderboard and the Top players boards are made up of real WordGlint players. When you play, the app sends our server your display name, your chosen avatar, a random player ID and secret token, and your scores: your best Classic, 5×5 and Daily Glint scores, and your weekly league total, together with the day or week (in your local calendar) they belong to. These are shown to other players on the boards. They are not linked to your name, email, device identifier or location, and are not used for advertising or analytics. Daily and weekly scores are deleted automatically after a few weeks. Your anonymous player record is deleted after 90 days without use.

On Android you may also choose to sign in to Google Play Games to post your scores to Google's leaderboards. That is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, and is optional.

Our server may briefly process your IP address to deliver the service and limit abuse, and standard server logs may contain it; we do not use it to identify you. The server is hosted with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

9. Children

WordGlint is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control of it. Deleting the app removes it. Match data on our server is deleted automatically after 48 hours, and your anonymous leaderboard and league record after 90 days without use. To have it removed sooner, email us your display name and approximate dates. See how to delete your data. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their policies, and you can manage subscriptions in your store account.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example Canada, the EU/UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we hold almost no personal data (only the display name, avatar and scores described above), there is usually little for us to provide, but you can contact us at any time with a request or a question.

12. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security. Online traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not run servers that hold your game progress.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date above, and where required, ask for your consent in the app.

14. Contact

Questions about privacy: wordglintsupport@wealthboardapp.com.
